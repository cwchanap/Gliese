# Quest 4: The Third Trial, the Boar King

Drogg joins Grik at the crossroads, "to carry the meat". At the Silverpine
falls his jokes turn into real advice, and the brothers bring down the Boar King.

::: story
id: prologue.trial-boar-king
chapter: prologue
map: meadow-entry
:::

::: dialogue
npc: drogg-crossroads
branch: objective:meet-drogg-crossroads
speaker: Drogg
:::

There he is. Father sent me to help carry the meat home. That's all. Not because he's worried.

[Grik] He's worried.

He's terrified. Don't tell him I said that either.

The Boar King roams Silverpine, north across the river. Old, huge, and meaner than Oren before breakfast.

Its trail starts past the bridge. Go and find it. I'll be right behind you.

::: dialogue
npc: drogg-crossroads
branch: objective:track-the-boar-king
speaker: Drogg
:::

North, across the bridge into Silverpine. Look for the trail. You can't miss it. Trees don't usually lie down on their own.

::: dialogue
npc: drogg-crossroads
branch: always
speaker: Drogg
:::

Silverpine is that way. The scary way.

::: scene
id: prologue.boar-king-trail
:::

The trail is not a trail so much as a road. Saplings are flattened, stones are overturned, and the earth is churned into ridges.

Each hoofprint is wider than Grik's two hands spread together.

[Grik] It went up toward the falls.

From somewhere ahead, over the noise of the water, comes a low, grinding snort.

::: dialogue
npc: drogg-falls
branch: objective:talk-to-drogg-falls
speaker: Drogg
:::

Hear that? It's up on the terrace. It knows we're here.

Listen, little brother. No jokes for once.

When a beast that big comes at you, every part of you will want to back away. Don't. You can't outrun it backwards.

Step to the side. Let it pass. Strike while it turns. That's all hunting is, really. Knowing where not to stand.

[Grik] Drogg. Thank you.

Don't make it strange. Go on. I'll be right here if it goes badly. Which it won't.

::: dialogue
npc: drogg-falls
branch: objective:defeat-boar-king
speaker: Drogg
:::

Up on the terrace. Sidestep, then strike. When it gets angry, and it will, keep your head.

::: dialogue
npc: drogg-falls
branch: always
speaker: Drogg
:::

Falls are loud. Good. Hides how hard my heart is beating.

::: scene
id: prologue.boar-king-felled
:::

The Boar King sways, groans, and crashes into the moss. The ground shakes once, and then the forest is quiet.

[Drogg] You did it. Grik, you actually did it!

[Drogg] Wait until Father sees this. Wait until the whole village sees this!

Drogg heaves the great boar across his shoulders, staggers, laughs, and sets off down the mountain.

[Drogg] Told you I came to carry the meat.

::: dialogue
npc: borak
map: guild-hall

The Boar King of Silverpine. My father faced it, and I faced it. Now it is your turn.

Drogg is waiting at the crossroads. Go.
