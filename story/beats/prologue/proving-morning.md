# Quest 1: The Proving Morning

Grik oversleeps on the morning of his proving hunt. Tikka drags him out of bed,
Old Mott hands down the family amulet, and Grik gathers his spear and herbs on
the way to Borak at the Hunters' Lodge.

Morning light slants through the gaps in the thatch. Somewhere outside, a rooster is losing an argument with a goat.

[Tikka] Grik. Grik! Get up!

[Grik] Mm. Five more breaths.

[Tikka] Today is your proving hunt, you lump! Father and Drogg left for the lodge before the sun was up.

[Tikka] If you make Father wait, he'll pretend he isn't angry. That's worse. Grandfather wants you by the hearth first. Go!

::: dialogue
npc: mott-hearth
branch: objective:talk-to-mott
speaker: Old Mott
:::

There he is. The last goblin in Sundrop to wake, on the one morning it matters.

Come here, boy. Bend down. My knees will not reach your neck.

This amulet was my father's, and his mother's before him. It has hung on every hunter in our family since the old days.

It will not stop a tusk. But it will remind you whose blood you carry. Wear it, and bring it home.

[Grik] I will, Grandfather.

Good. Oren has the spear your father ordered, and Mira has your herb pouch. Fetch them both before you go to Borak.

::: dialogue
npc: tikka-home
branch: quest:prologue-proving-morning

Why are you still here? Spear from Oren, herbs from Mira, then the lodge. Even I know that, and I'm not the one being proved.

::: dialogue

Borak's boy! Your father paid for this a week ago and made me swear not to tell you.

A practice spear. Ash shaft, iron head, balanced for a short arm. No offence.

[Grik] Some offence.

Ha! Grow into it. Open your pack and equip it before you leave. A spear in a bag has never killed anything.

If you come home with coin, come back. I keep better steel for real hunters.

Your herb pouch, hunter-to-be. Three field potions, mixed this morning.

Drink one when a wound will not close on its own. Do not wait until you are seeing two of everything.

Lirra was up before dawn too. She said she needed herbs from the Wildwood edge. Today. Of all days.

I'm sure it's a coincidence.

Your spear is at Oren's forge, your herbs are at Mira's hut. Then come back.

Grik. You came.

Every hunter of Sundrop has stood where you stand. Today you face the Three Trials of the Hunter.

The first: read the land. A thornback boar has been tearing up the Wildwood. Find its trail and bring it down.

The second: feed the village. A hunter fills the stores, not just his own belly.

The third: face the king of beasts. When you reach it, you will know.

[Drogg] Don't worry, Father. If he comes back with a rabbit, we'll call it a very small boar.

[Borak] Drogg.

[Drogg] Quiet. Yes.

[Borak] The Wildwood is east, past the river. Go, and come back a hunter.
