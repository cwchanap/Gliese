# Quest 6: The Morning of Fire

War horns before dawn. Humans in sun-crested armour are in the village. Borak
falls at the gate, Drogg at the well, and Tikka and Lirra are taken. Grik
charges the captain and loses.

A horn. Then another, long and low, nothing like the hunting horns of Sundrop.

Smoke is coming in under the door. Outside, someone is screaming.

[Old Mott] Grik! Up! Up, boy!
