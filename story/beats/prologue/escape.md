# Quest 7: The Old Tunnel

Grik wakes in the shrine. Old Mott opens the sealed tunnel and stays behind to
close it. Grik crosses the tunnel, climbs to the ridge above the Wildwood cave,
and makes his vow.

::: dialogue

Easy. Easy, boy. You are in the shrine. I carried you here myself, so don't tell me I'm too old.

[Grik] Father. Drogg. They...

I know.

[Grik] Tikka and Lirra. They took them. I have to...

You have to live. That is all you have to do tonight.

The humans are searching every house. They will reach the shrine before the fire burns out.

Do you remember what I told you last night? The tunnel under the shrine. I told you no one goes in. Tonight, you do.

It runs under the mountain and comes out in the Wildwood, past the old cave. Our ancestors dug it, when the Great Hunt came the first time.

::: dialogue
npc: mott-shrine
branch: always
speaker: Old Mott
:::

The tunnel is waiting, boy. Go.

::: scene
id: prologue.tunnel-seals
:::

Old Mott pulls a carved stone from the shrine floor. Cold air breathes up out of the dark.

[Grik] Come with me.

[Old Mott] Someone has to close the door behind you. I am the only one left who knows how.

[Old Mott] Live, Grik. Remember us.

[Old Mott] Now, this is your hunt.

Grik steps down into the dark. Behind him, stone grinds on stone. Then the ceiling roars, and the tunnel falls in.

::: scene
id: prologue.ridge-overlook
:::

The cave opens onto a high ridge above the Wildwood. Far below, beyond the river, Sundrop is a smudge of black smoke against the dawn.

On the southern road, a long line of soldiers marches away from the village. Between them, roped together, walk the goblins of Sundrop.

He can't see Tikka or Lirra. He looks until his eyes hurt.

::: scene
id: prologue.the-vow
:::

Grik's hand finds something in his pack. Straight shaft. Iron head. A small golden sun stamped into the fletching.

Not an orc arrow. It never was.

[Grik] They hunted us like beasts.

[Grik] Now I will hunt them.
