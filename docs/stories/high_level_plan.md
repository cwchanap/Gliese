# Gliese — Story Direction

> Status: direction reset (2026-10-24). Replaces the earlier modern-Tokyo concept, which
> lives on in git history. Names marked _(working)_ are placeholders: change them freely.

## 1. Logline

Grik is a young goblin hunter in Sundrop, a hidden village deep in the Wildwood. The
day after his first real hunt, a human troop burns Sundrop, kills the men of his
family, and carries off his sister and the girl he loves. Grik escapes and swears that
the humans who hunt goblins will learn what it is to be hunted.

## 2. Pillars

- **Hunter and hunted.** The prologue opens with Grik hunting animals to feed his
  village and ends with him vowing to hunt humans. The whole arc keeps asking which one
  he is becoming.
- **Small against big.** Goblins were once the largest race on the island, but one goblin
  is small and weak next to an armoured human. Grik wins by knowing the land, by tricks
  and by friends, not by raw strength.
- **Warm before cruel.** The village has to feel like home before it burns. Spend real
  time on everyday family life in the prologue so the raid lands.
- **JRPG-framed darkness.** The violence is real but not shown in detail. Cut away, use
  silhouettes and aftermath, and keep it off the screen. Nothing sexual happens to the
  captives.

## 3. World

### 3.1 The island

The story happens on one island continent: Gliese _(working)_. It is big enough for
several peoples and far enough from the human mainland that nobody had seen humans
there until recently.

### 3.2 Peoples

| People  | Then                                                                           | Now                                                                                       |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Goblins | The largest people on the island, living in villages spread through the woods. | Hunted. Scattered villages hide deep in the forest. Sundrop is one of the last at peace. |
| Orcs    | Hill clans and goblins' closest trading partners.                              | Hunted alongside goblins. Their strongholds are burning one by one.                       |
| Elves   | Reclusive forest and coast folk who kept to themselves.                        | Stayed neutral and so far untouched. Their loyalty is unclear.                            |
| Demons  | Proud and few, ruling the northern highlands.                                  | Mostly wiped out by the Hero. Only scattered survivors remain.                            |
| Humans  | Not on the island at all.                                                      | Arrived with the Hero, built a walled city on the south coast, and are pushing inland.    |

### 3.3 Timeline

Years are counted back from the prologue and are suggestions only.

- **The Long Peace (centuries).** Goblins, orcs, elves and demons share the island.
  There are trades, feuds and treaties, but no wars of extermination. The ancestral goblin
  ruins near Sundrop date from this era.
- **The Hero's Landing (about 12 years ago).** A human Hero arrives by sea with an army,
  calling it a crusade against the demons.
- **The Fall of the Demons (about 12 to 9 years ago).** The Hero breaks the demon
  highlands. Most demons die, and the rest scatter.
- **The Human City (about 9 years ago to now).** Humans found a walled city on the south
  coast, called Valcrest _(working)_.
- **The Hunts (ongoing).** Human companies range inland and hunt goblins and orcs.
  Goblins only hear about it from refugees and rumours. Sundrop, hidden in the Wildwood,
  has been spared so far.

## 4. Cast

### 4.1 Grik — protagonist

A young goblin who is about to become a full hunter of Sundrop. He is quick, stubborn
and sharp-eyed, and a little too eager to prove himself to his father and older brother.
He is a better tracker than fighter.

- **Wants:** at first, to be counted among the village hunters. After the raid, revenge,
  and to get Tikka and Lirra back.
- **Wound:** he ran while his family died, even though Old Mott made him go. His guilt
  drives the revenge.
- **Arc question:** whether a goblin who learns to hunt humans stays a goblin of Sundrop
  or becomes the very thing that burned it.

### 4.2 Grik's family

The male family dies in the raid. Only Grik survives.

| Name                   | Role                                                                                                    | Fate in the raid                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Borak _(working)_      | Father. Hunt Chief of Sundrop, gruff and proud, and the one who sends Grik on his first hunt.          | Killed holding the village gate.                                         |
| Drogg _(working)_      | Older brother. The best fighter among the young hunters, who teases Grik constantly.                   | Killed trying to reach Tikka.                                            |
| Old Mott _(working)_   | Grandfather and the village elder. He keeps the shrine and tells stories of the Long Peace.            | Sends Grik down the old tunnels and stays behind to seal them.           |
| Tikka _(working)_      | Younger sister. Bright, bossy, and the one who runs the household.                                      | Taken alive by the humans.                                               |

Grik's mother died some winters ago, which is why Tikka keeps the house. _(Default
choice. See open questions.)_

### 4.3 Lirra _(working)_ — Grik's girlfriend

A herb gatherer and the best tracker of their generation, better than Grik, and she
never lets him forget it. She joins him on the first hunt. She is taken alive with
Tikka.

### 4.4 Humans

- **The Hero.** He is a legend to the humans and a monster to everyone else. Grik never
  meets him in the prologue. He is the far goal of the revenge arc.
- **The Sun-Crest Captain.** The officer who leads the raid on Sundrop. Grik only knows
  him by the silver sun crest on his helm. He is the first revenge target and the
  prologue's forced-loss fight. He gets a name once Grik learns it.
- **Human soldiers.** Ordinary troopers in the raid, and the first humans Grik fights.

## 5. Story arc

The prologue is planned scene by scene in [`prologue.md`](./prologue.md). Everything
after it is direction only, sketched so the prologue's hooks have somewhere to land.

| Part      | Shape                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prologue  | Peaceful Sundrop, the first hunt, a family evening, the raid, the escape and the vow.                                                                                                                       |
| Chapter 1 | Survival in the burned Wildwood. Grik tracks the troop's trail, finds other goblin survivors, and takes his first ambush revenge on a straggler patrol.                                                     |
| Chapter 2 | Allies. Orc clans under siege, a cautious elf enclave and a hiding demon survivor. Each wants something different from a goblin with a grudge.                                                              |
| Chapter 3 | Valcrest. Grik learns where the captives were taken and why, faces the Sun-Crest Captain, and gets his first look at the Hero.                                                                              |
| Later     | The Hero and the question of what revenge costs. The ending is still open.                                                                                                                                  |

## 6. Open questions

Each one has a default, so writing can go ahead without waiting for an answer.

1. **Grik's mother.** Default: she died before the story starts. The alternatives are
   that she dies in the raid or that she is a captive too.
2. **Why the humans take captives.** Default: Grik doesn't know yet, and it is revealed
   in Chapter 3. Possibilities include labour, bait for other villages, or something the
   Hero needs.
3. **Are all humans villains?** Default: the prologue shows only the troop. Whether Grik
   later meets humans who complicate the vow is up to later chapters.
4. **Working names.** Grik, Borak, Drogg, Old Mott, Tikka, Lirra, Gliese (the island) and
   Valcrest are all placeholders.
